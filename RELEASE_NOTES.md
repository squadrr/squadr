**What's new in 0.2.5**

- **Clips.** Squadr can keep the last moments of your screen and game audio, and save them as a video when you press a keybind (Alt+F10 by default, change it in Settings). Clips go to Videos\Squadr Clips or any folder you pick, and the new Clips page lets you watch, open and delete them. The first time you turn it on, Squadr downloads FFmpeg, the free recording engine (about 80 MB, once). It records with your graphics card's encoder, so it's light on your games.
- **Liquid glass.** A frosted, see-through look for panels. Turn it on in Settings > Interface. Works with Classic and Pulse.
- **Transparent background.** Let your desktop show through, blurred, with Windows Acrylic or Mica. Settings > Interface.

Download **Squadr_*_x64-setup.exe** below and run it. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Already have Squadr 0.2.1 or later? It updates itself: look for **Update** in the status bar or in Settings.
