**What's new in 0.2.4**

- **Fixed: adding a note when you ask to join gave "Xbox Live error 400".** Xbox join requests can't carry text, so your request now goes through on its own and your note is sent to the host as an Xbox message.
- **Spam filter.** Posts and message requests selling followers or gamertags (like the "WWW.X6RR.COM" ads written in look-alike letters) are hidden.
- **New home.** Squadr now lives at github.com/squadrr/squadr, and has a website: https://squadrr.github.io/squadr/. Updates come from the new address.

Download **Squadr_*_x64-setup.exe** below and run it. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Already have Squadr 0.2.1 or later? It updates itself: look for **Update** in the status bar or in Settings.
