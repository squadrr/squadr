**What's new in 0.3.0**

- **LFG alerts.** Tell Squadr what you're looking for (a game, words like "ranked plat", tags, group size) and it pings you when a matching group goes up, even while it's in the tray. Make one on the new Alerts page, or press **Alert me** on any game.
- **Templates.** Save a post you use a lot and put it up again with one click from New post.
- **Auto-repost.** Turn on **Repost automatically** when you post, and if your post ends before it fills, Squadr puts it back up.
- **Players.** Everyone you've grouped with through Squadr, with a thumbs up/down and notes so you remember who was good to play with. Their posts and requests get a "Played 3×" badge, and people you rated down are left out of your alerts.
- **Share clips.** Send a clip to a friend or chat, or copy a link. Clips are uploaded to catbox.moe, as a permanent link or one that expires in 3 days.
- **Runs in the tray.** Closing the window keeps Squadr running next to the clock, so your posts and alerts stay live. The tray icon gets a red dot when someone wants to join, you have a message or an alert matches. Quit from the tray icon or File > Exit.
- **Start with Windows and notifications.** Squadr can start quietly with your PC, and shows Windows notifications for join requests, getting picked, messages and alerts when it isn't in front. Both are in Settings > Background.
- **Fewer crashes.** A problem in one part of Squadr no longer takes the whole app down.

Download **Squadr_*_x64-setup.exe** below and run it. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Already have Squadr 0.2.1 or later? It updates itself: look for **Update** in the status bar or in Settings.
