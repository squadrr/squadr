**What's new in 0.2.6**

- **Crash reports.** If Squadr runs into a problem and has to close, it now tells you why instead of just disappearing, and saves the details to `%APPDATA%\com.squadr.app\crash.log`. If Squadr crashes on your PC, please send that file (or a screenshot of the message) in the Discord so it can be fixed.

Download **Squadr_*_x64-setup.exe** below and run it. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Already have Squadr 0.2.1 or later? It updates itself: look for **Update** in the status bar or in Settings.
