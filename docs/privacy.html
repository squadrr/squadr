Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Squadr is a Windows app that lets you use Xbox Looking for Group, messages and parties from your PC. This page explains what the app stores, what it sends, and to whom. The short version: Squadr has no servers of its own, collects no analytics, and never sees your Microsoft password.

Signing in

You sign in on Microsoft's own website (microsoft.com/link) with a one-time code. Microsoft then gives Squadr sign-in tokens for your Xbox account. Squadr never sees or stores your password.

The tokens are saved on your PC, encrypted with your Windows account (Windows DPAPI), so other Windows users on the same PC can't read them. Signing out in Squadr deletes them.

What Squadr stores on your PC

  • Your encrypted sign-in tokens.
  • Your settings: language, layout, pinned games and similar choices.
  • The LFG posts you're hosting or have asked to join, so Squadr can take your post down and withdraw your requests when you close it.
  • Small caches, such as which Xbox version matches a PC game and how many posts a game has.

All of this is in Squadr's folder in your Windows AppData. Uninstalling Squadr and deleting that folder removes it.

Who Squadr talks to

  • Microsoft and Xbox Live. To sign you in, and to load and change LFG posts, messages, friends, presence and parties on your behalf. What you post, send and join is visible to other Xbox players the same way it would be from an Xbox console or the Xbox app. Microsoft's own privacy statement covers that data.
  • GitHub. To check for updates, download new versions, and read the list of community links (Discord, YouTube, TikTok). These requests don't include any of your Xbox information.

Squadr doesn't use analytics, ads, trackers or crash reporting, and doesn't send your data to the people who make Squadr.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies or analytics. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement. The page asks GitHub's API for the latest version so the download button points to the newest installer.

Children

Squadr follows your Xbox account's settings. If your account can't use Looking for Group or messages because of family settings, Squadr can't either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.

Squadr is an independent app. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Microsoft or Xbox.
